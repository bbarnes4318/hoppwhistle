Audio Pipeline

Rhythm is Everything

In music, if you're off-beat, you lose the groove. In AI voice, if you have high latency, you lose the listener. Hopwhistle's Resonate Engine is tuned for musical timing.

Ingestion & VAD

Silero VAD v5 detects speech in <10ms. It knows exactly when a fan stops talking or interrupts with excitement, ensuring the agent never "talks over" the applause.

CORE ENGINE

Inference (vLLM)

The L40S GPU runs vLLM with FP8 quantization. The "Brain" and the "Mouth" share VRAM. Tokens are streamed speculatively, allowing the artist agent to improvise responses instantly.

Streaming Synthesis

Kokoro ONNX synthesizes audio chunks instantly. By the time the LLM finishes a thought, the fan has already heard the hook. No buffering, just flow.
